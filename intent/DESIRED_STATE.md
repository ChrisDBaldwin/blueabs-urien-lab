# Desired State

## Vision: The Golden Road

Urien Lab is a guided path from "I picked Urien" to "I can play Urien." Not a reference tool, not a sandbox — a tutorial that teaches you everything in the right order.

The goal is progressive accretion. Each exercise builds on the last. The player learns game systems and mechanics *in the order they need them* to execute Urien's combos. If the progression is clear and the ordering is right, a player who follows the road comes out the other side able to play.

## The Road

1. **Fundamentals** — What is charge? What is canceling? What is linking? Exercises that isolate each mechanic before combining them.
2. **Basic confirms** — Simple hit-confirms into specials. One cancel window, one charge direction.
3. **Intermediate combos** — Multi-hit sequences, tackle loops, charge partitioning.
4. **Advanced techniques** — Aegis unblockable setups, multi-combo sequences, corner carry.
5. **Matchup application** — What works on who, and when. Urien's punishes are layered — what connects on one character whiffs on another, and the situations where each combo applies don't line up the way you'd expect.

## Priorities

### 1. Tutorial Curriculum

A deliberate, ordered progression through Urien's toolkit.

- **Exercises ordered into a path.** A player opening the menu sees a clear starting point and a clear next step. Difficulty ratings serve the path, not just the sort order.
- **No gaps in the difficulty curve.** Every jump in difficulty is small enough that the player who mastered the previous step can attempt the next one.
- **Mechanics before combos.** Exercises that isolate a single system (charge timing, cancel windows, link timing) before asking the player to combine them.
- **Visible progression.** The player sees where they are on the road — what's mastered, what's next, what's ahead.

### 2. Matchup Curriculum

Playing Urien well means knowing what you can do to your opponent and when. Combos that work on one character whiff on another. Setups that are airtight in one matchup have gaps in a different one. The matchup curriculum reveals this — punish exercises tagged per opponent, with save states that put you in the right situation against the right character.

### 3. Community Exercise Exchange

Players record combos and share them with low friction.

- **Submit** — Upload exercise definitions to a central location.
- **Review** — Curation before exercises go live. Manual is fine to start.
- **Distribute** — Sync pulls approved community exercises without overwriting user content.
- **Attribution** — Credit contributors in exercise metadata.

Third priority because community exercises need a curriculum to land in, not just a list to append to.

---

## Not Planned

- **Defensive exercises (parry training)** — Other training mode tools handle this better. Urien Lab isn't trying to replace training mode — it's a guided tutorial for Urien's offense.
- **Multi-character support** — Urien Lab teaches Urien. Fork the repo and build an Alex Lab or a Dudley Lab.
